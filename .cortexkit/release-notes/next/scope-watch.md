AFT now follows scope endings through the daemon's `scope.watch` feed, replacing the 60-second scope polling and five-second grant sweep for open watches. Each ending is confirmed with `scope.describe` before stopping shell tasks, deleting saved grants, or settling held calls. End notifications are retained even when another confirmation is already running, and watch snapshots also trigger confirmation. Healthy watches receive staggered verification at a fifteen-minute cadence to catch missing pushes. Older daemons and interrupted or opening watches retain polling until snapshots arrive; reconnects and live epoch changes alone never revoke a scope. Health includes watch coverage, verification counts, and counts of malformed saved-grant or held-call records skipped during scope bookkeeping.
