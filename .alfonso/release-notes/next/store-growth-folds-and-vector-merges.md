- AFT's index store stays small in long use. A worktree that stopped publishing its changes used to keep every segment it read, so idle worktrees could pin gigabytes; now, when another checkout of the repository publishes, it copies what the idle one still reads into one new segment and drops the rest, with no rebuild. Meaning-search vectors, which each batch of embeddings stored in a segment of its own, are now combined once a repository has more than eight such segments, and searches answer exactly as before. On copies of two real stores, segments went from 213 to 30 (5.2 GB to 1.9 GB) and from 335 to 20 (1.6 GB to 0.6 GB).
