Runner sessions can acknowledge their own background-command completions through the private `bash_ack_completions` transport tool, without adding a model-visible tool. Thalamus runner completions now remain pending until acknowledged and can be offered on up to three route bindings in total, with a durable offer count and a 24-hour expiry; exhausted or expired completions are retired and logged. Other runner principals retain once-only delivery. Completion offer and retirement writes stay off the route runtime thread.
