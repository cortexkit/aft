- The health rollup and the module loop's allocator-slack sampler now wait longer between allocator statistics walks when a walk is expensive. The wait is at least 200 times the last walk's duration, never less than before (30 s for the rollup, 60 s for the sampler) and never more than 10 minutes. On Linux, glibc's `mallinfo2` visits every free chunk while holding that arena's lock. On a fragmented heap with 5-7 GiB freed in small blocks it took 0.65-6.5 s per walk, so walking every 30 s cost up to 20% of a core and stalled allocation in other threads. On macOS a walk on a similar heap takes 16-27 ms, so the interval there stays as it was. Memory replies are unchanged: `allocator_observation_age_ms` still reports how old the reused reading is.
